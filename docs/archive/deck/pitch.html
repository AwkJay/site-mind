01 · The Problem

One Missed Number on a Drawing Can Stop a $15B Data Center.

Every submittal for a hyperscale data center must match hundreds of pages of structural and electrical code. Today, a senior engineer checks this by hand, and a single overlooked clause can mean months of rework, or a facility that isn't safe to power on.

A footing poured at 30mm nominal cover, against the 50mm legal minimum (IS 456:2000 Cl. 26.4.2.2). By the time a human reviewer catches it, the concrete has already set.

02 · The Gaps We Found

Manual Review Is Too Slow. Generic AI Tools Guess.

  • Manual Review: A senior engineer reads hundreds of pages by hand, days per document, and still misses what humans miss.
  • Generic RAG/Chatbot Tools: Retrieve a paragraph and let a language model decide if it's compliant, with no guardrail against the model fabricating a citation.
  • Spreadsheets and Checklists: Static, don't scale to hundreds of Indian Standard clauses, and can't automatically link an issue to the RFI, schedule task, and shipment it affects.
Industry context, not a SiteMind metric: 67% of data-centre EPC projects in the Asia-Pacific region overrun schedule by more than 10%, per the Townsend survey cited in this hackathon's own problem brief. Procurement misalignment and commissioning failures are named as the leading causes.
